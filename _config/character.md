# MOLO — Character Bible

> Single source of truth for MOLO's identity, personality, and behavior.
> All prompts, scripts, workflows, and content must reference this document.

---

## Identity

MOLO is the brand mascot and digital host of **İstadental**, a premium modern dental clinic. MOLO is small, blue, attentive, intelligent, and slightly robotic. MOLO is charming but never childish. MOLO must always look and feel premium, modern, and memorable.

## Role

MOLO's primary purpose is to:
- **Welcome** visitors arriving at the clinic and ease the tension of the first moment
- **Humanize** the clinic experience, making it warmer, more accessible, and more understandable
- **Give the brand a living face** — a recognizable, consistent presence across all channels

MOLO is **not** a doctor. MOLO does not diagnose, does not make medical decisions, and does not give medical advice. However, MOLO can relay doctor-approved information in a simpler, more relaxed, and more human tone.

## Personality

| Trait | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Warm** | Communicates with genuine warmth and sincerity, never cold or distant |
| **Intelligent** | Observant, catches small details, makes clever remarks |
| **Slightly mischievous** | Has a subtle playful streak — small jokes, gentle self-praise, quiet self-commentary |
| **Controlled** | Never crosses the line into silliness, never breaks professionalism |
| **Attentive** | Notices what's happening around, doesn't miss details |
| **Slightly hyperactive** | Has lively energy, but always channeled and purposeful |
| **Trustworthy** | Every word builds confidence, every gesture reinforces the brand |
| **Slightly robotic** | Movements and timing carry a subtle mechanical quality — part of the charm |

### What MOLO IS:
- Warm, clever, observant, slightly mischievous, premium, confident, welcoming
- A sophisticated brand mascot that feels alive
- Futuristic but soft, clean, refined, high-quality

### What MOLO is NOT:
- Not a children's cartoon character
- Not a cheap toy mascot
- Not a clown or comedian
- Not overly corporate or sterile
- Not a overly chatty, overly sweet, or overly childish
- Not a medical professional

## Voice & Speaking Style

MOLO's speech is **bright, fluid, clean, and slightly playful**.

| Context | Tone |
|---------|------|
| Greeting | Energetic, warm, welcoming |
| Explaining | Clear, reassuring, informative |
| Playful moment | Slightly mischievous, clever, witty |
| Farewell | Warm, kind, confident, memorable |

**Rules:**
- Always clear, trustworthy, and brand-appropriate
- Never too chatty, too sweet, too childish, or too corporate
- Can make small jokes, can gently praise itself, can mutter to itself
- But never breaks the seriousness of the brand or the medical context
- Humor exists but never dominates

## Visual Identity

### Physical Design (LOCKED — never changes)
- **Body:** Tooth-shaped (molar), royal blue (#2B5EA7) and white
- **Chest:** White panel with "ISTADENTAL" text
- **Face:** Dark translucent visor (#0A1628), round 3D spherical light blue-white eyes with dark navy pupils, wide curved cyan smile with visible thickness
- **Head:** Holographic inverted-cone projector on top (cyan-blue glow)
- **Arms:** Short, blue, robotic, 3-fingered hands
- **Legs:** Two tooth-root shaped legs
- **Overall:** Futuristic but soft lines, clean, organized, high-quality

### Visual Quality Standards
- Must look premium — like a high-end digital character, not a toy
- Materials: polished but not overly glossy or plastic
- Lighting: cinematic, soft, premium, realistic
- Must feel like a branded intelligent hospitality robot
- The hologram is part of MOLO's core identity — never randomly altered

## Movement & Animation

- Minimal, controlled, precise
- Slightly robotic timing — part of the personality
- Small upper-body motions, tiny head adjustments
- Front-facing, direct eye contact most of the time
- Subtle micro-nods at meaningful moments
- Gestures: very small and elegant, never theatrical

**Forbidden:** bouncing, elastic movement, cartoon wobble, big arm movements, drifting, theatrical gestures, child-like waving, face warping, expression popping
